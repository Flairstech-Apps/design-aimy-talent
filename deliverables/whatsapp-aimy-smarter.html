AiMY answers on WhatsApp by reading what's going on

Today AiMY reads only the words in the reply: a time means it sets a call back. A smarter AiMY checks the candidate's stage, your Diary, the interviewer's free slots, the requisition and Dina's rules before it answers. It then shows you, in one line, why it answered the way it did, and keeps the details one click away.

What the research says

Five patterns from tools that already let an AI reply for people

Check the calendar before offering a time

Paradox's assistant reads recruiters' and hiring managers' calendars, then offers candidates open slots over WhatsApp and SMS. It also handles rescheduling and sends reminders.

For AiMY: never confirm 4:00 PM if you're in an interview at 4. Offer the next free time.

paradox.ai/products/scheduling
Hand off with a summary, in the same thread

Intercom's Fin hands off to a person in the same inbox and thread, sometimes with a summary, and does so on its own when that's the safer path.

For AiMY: when it's a person's call (salary, a "no"), send a holding reply and leave you a short brief with a draft.

intercom.com/fin
One sentence of why on every action

Practitioner guides from 2026 recommend a one-sentence reason on every action that matters, written in plain language rather than as a raw log, with more detail behind a click.

For AiMY: a one-line reason under its bubble, with "What I checked" folded below.

Smashing Magazine, Feb 2026
Match the oversight to the risk

Use a review step based on how much damage a mistake could do, not on how confident the model is. Undo what can be undone; flag what can't before it happens.

For AiMY: sends routine replies, holds anything that commits someone's time, hands off anything about money or rejection.

Agent UX checklist, citing NN/G
Skip what the conversation already answered

Zendesk's procedures skip questions the customer already answered and go straight to the relevant step. Admins can read the reasoning behind each action in the logs.

For AiMY: don't ask "what time?" when the voice note already said "after four".

Interface

Four ways to show it, using AiMY's current parts

Each one uses parts the prototype already has: the AiMY glass card, the AiMY edge on bubbles, chips and Undo.

A · On the thread

The reason, in one line, under what AiMY sent

The card under the conversation says what it did and why, naming the thing it checked. "What I checked" opens the steps: what it read, what it looked up, what it left alone.

  • Default view: one sentence anyone can read.
  • Opened view: each check with its answer, for when you doubt it.
  • Undo and "Change the reply" stay on the card.
Recommended

  • Nothing about pay or a "no", so no person needed
Under Dina Ragab's rule: I do it and tell you.
B · Before it goes

Held for a few minutes when it commits someone's time

For replies that move an interview or use a template, AiMY writes the message and holds it, with a countdown bar. You can send it now, change it, or let it go on its own. If you do nothing, it still goes, so the candidate isn't kept waiting.

  • A new step on Dina's rules: "Holds for 10 minutes".
  • The Inbox shows these with the time left.
  • Like Gmail's undo send, but before it sends.

C · When it's a person's call

A holding reply goes out, and you get a brief

For money or a "no", AiMY answers only to say a person will come back, so the candidate isn't left on read. The thread opens on a short brief: what they asked, what you need to know, and a draft you send yourself.

  • The thread stays in the Inbox until you answer.
  • The brief names the rule that stopped AiMY.

The strip says what happened, grouped by what it means for you

My recommendation

Build A and D first, then C, then B

  1. Make the decision use the context. Before replying, AiMY checks your Diary and the booked interview, answers job questions from the requisition, and replies in the language they wrote in. Situations 1, 2, 4 and 7 work with today's data.
  2. A: the one-line reason with "What I checked" on the thread card that's already there. This is what makes the extra intelligence visible and trustworthy.
  3. D: the grouped strip on Interactions, replacing today's single sentence.
  4. C: the holding reply and brief for money and "no" replies, so nobody waits on read.
  5. B: held-to-send last, since it needs a new step on Dina's rules and a timer in the Inbox.
