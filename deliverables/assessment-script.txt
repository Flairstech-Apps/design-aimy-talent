AiMY Talent · Assessment script
[DO] = what to do on screen · [MOTION] = the animation to point out

1 · What it is  (0:00 · 20 s)

[DO] Point at the top of the page.

This is one assessment: support troubleshooting and written English. It's a short questionnaire a candidate takes from a link, before the phone screen. It takes about twenty minutes, and the pass mark is seventy percent.

I copy the link and send it. AiMY scores it, and a person decides.


2 · Before anyone takes it  (0:20 · 45 s)

[DO] Point at "What keeps people finishing, and keeps it fair". Point at "3 of 5".

Our research found five things that keep people finishing a test, and keep it fair. This assessment does three of them.

• Done: it works on a phone, and it asks for consent before AiMY scores it. Anyone can ask a person to score it instead.

• Done: there's a practice question first that doesn't count.

• Not yet: it's twenty minutes, and past fifteen more people give up. And nobody hears how they did.

Each fix is one press.

[DO] Press "Turn it on" next to "Nobody hears how they did".

[MOTION] The count goes from 3 of 5 to 4 of 5, and a toast confirms it with Undo.

[DO] Press Undo.


3 · Which questions work  (1:05 · 1 min)

[DO] Scroll to "Item insights".

Sixty-five people have taken it. For each question, we see how many got it right, and whether getting it right relates to what happened next, like the client saying yes.

[DO] Point at Q2.

Question two predicts the client's yes. People who get it right go further. That's a question worth keeping.

[DO] Point at Q5 and the AiMY line under the table.

Question five is the opposite. Seventy-four percent get it wrong, and it predicts nothing. When nearly everyone misses one question, the question is usually the problem.

So AiMY proposes a clearer wording. The HR Manager approves it, or I keep the question as it is. Nothing changes on its own.


4 · The questions  (2:05 · 30 s)

[DO] Scroll to Questions. On Q2, press "Earlier wording".

Each question shows the right answer and how many got it right.

[MOTION] The earlier wording opens smoothly, like an accordion.

And when a question changes, we keep its history. Question two used to be free text, and most people got it wrong. The HR Manager approved turning it into a choice, testing the same skill.


5 · Results  (2:35 · 25 s)

[DO] Scroll to Results.

At the bottom is everyone who took it, newest first: their score, pass or not, and the role.

Some carry an integrity flag, for example an answer that was pasted in. It's shown on its own line, and it never changes the score. A person looks at it.

You can also see who asked for a person to score it, or asked for a review. That's the rule everywhere in Talent: AiMY scores, and a person decides.
