When it’s your call

Pay and a “no” stay with you

A holding reply

AiMY only says you’ll come back today, so nobody is left on read.

A brief

The band, what they expect, their stage, and who else is waiting.

A draft you send

Use my draft, call them instead, or take back the holding reply.

Some replies are never AiMY’s to answer. When Adham asks about the salary range, or when Ayman says they’ve accepted another offer, AiMY doesn’t decide anything. It sends a short holding reply, “Omar will come back to you on that today”, so the candidate isn’t left on read. Then it leaves you a brief: the band on the requisition, what the candidate said they expect and whether that’s inside the band, their stage, and for a “no”, who else needs to hear, like the client. There’s a draft ready, and you send it yourself.
