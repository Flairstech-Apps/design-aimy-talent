AiMY answers the routine WhatsApp replies, and tells you why.

It checks what’s going on first. Anything that is really your call, it leaves to you.

Now the new part. Most WhatsApp replies are routine: “after 4 works”, “can we talk tomorrow morning?”, “is the role hybrid?”. Answering them takes a recruiter away from real work, but leaving them makes the candidate wait. So AiMY now answers those routine replies itself, under a rule the HR Manager sets. The important bit is that it checks what’s going on before it answers, and it tells you why it answered the way it did. Anything that’s really your decision, it leaves to you.
