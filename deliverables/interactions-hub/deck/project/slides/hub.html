This is the Interactions page: one page with three tabs. To reach is who you should call next. Inbox is what came in while you were away: missed calls and WhatsApp replies. The small AiMY mark next to Inbox counts the replies AiMY already answered for you. History is everything that happened, filed on each candidate’s record. Above the tabs there’s a strip from AiMY that tells you what it answered for you and what’s waiting on you. I’ll come back to that strip in a minute.
