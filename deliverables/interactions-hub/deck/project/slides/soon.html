Sending soon: it goes by itself, unless you step in

Why it goes on its own: the 24-hour rule

WhatsApp Business allows a free reply only within 24 hours of the candidate’s last message. After that, only an approved template. Sending on its own keeps the reply inside the window.

Source: Meta, WhatsApp Business Platform, “Send messages”
developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messages/send-messages

WhatsApp Business or your own: both in AiMY Talent

Through AiMY Connect, AiMY sends for you under the rule. With your own WhatsApp there’s no window: you send it, and Talent logs it.

The second group is Sending soon. Here AiMY has written a reply, but it doesn’t send it straight away. Mohamed asked to move his interview, and AiMY offered two of Tarek’s interview hours. Because that changes a colleague’s week, AiMY holds the reply for 10 minutes. If you do nothing, it goes. That matters because of a rule from WhatsApp itself: on WhatsApp Business, you can only send a free reply within 24 hours of the candidate’s last message. After that, only a template WhatsApp approved in advance. That’s in Meta’s own documentation, linked here. So a reply that waits for a busy recruiter can miss the window; sending on its own keeps it inside. And companies choose how they use WhatsApp: through AiMY Connect, which is WhatsApp Business, or each recruiter’s own WhatsApp. Both work in AiMY Talent. With your own WhatsApp there’s no 24-hour window: Talent writes the message, you send it, and it’s logged on the thread.
