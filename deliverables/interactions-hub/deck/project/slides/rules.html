What AiMY does, and what it never touches

Does, and tells you

  • Confirms a time you’re free
  • Offers your next free times
  • Answers from the requisition
  • Reopens a chat with a template, held first

Always yours

  • Pay
  • A “no”
  • A client’s time
So, in one view: what AiMY does on its own, and what it never touches. It confirms a time when you’re free, offers your next free times when you’re not, answers questions from the requisition, and reopens a chat with an approved template, which it holds first so you can step in. And three things are always yours: pay, a “no”, and a client’s time. The HR Manager sets how far AiMY goes with WhatsApp on the “What AiMY may do” page, from only suggesting to doing it and telling you.
