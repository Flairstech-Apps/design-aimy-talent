To reach is your call list for the day. AiMY puts people who asked for a callback first, inside the window they gave, then everyone who applied and hasn’t been called yet. Each row tells you what to get from the call, based on what the CV doesn’t say, like their notice period or a must-have skill. You can call one by one or press “Call all in order”. You still press Call each time; AiMY logs what happened.
