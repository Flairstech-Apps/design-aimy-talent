Source: Meta, WhatsApp Business Platform, “Send messages”. Without AiMY Connect, recruiters use their own WhatsApp: no window, no templates.

One rule comes from WhatsApp itself, not from us. On the WhatsApp Business Platform, once 24 hours have passed since the candidate’s last message, a business can only send a template that WhatsApp approved in advance. So templates matter. The HR Manager manages them on the Templates page: add one, delete one, and each new template waits for WhatsApp’s review before anyone can use it. Once 24 hours have passed, AiMY picks an approved template and holds it with a countdown, as we saw on the strip. If you’d rather send another, “Pick another template” opens the picker with every approved one. If you need a different one, “Ask for one” sends the request straight to the HR Manager. If a company chooses to let recruiters use their own WhatsApp instead of AiMY Connect, there’s no window and no templates at all.
