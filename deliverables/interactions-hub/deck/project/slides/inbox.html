The Inbox is everything that came in while you were away. A missed call comes with its voicemail already transcribed, and a Call them back button. A WhatsApp reply shows its status in plain words, like “they wrote, I said you’ll reply”. When you call back or reply, the row clears itself. The small AiMY mark on the tab tells you how many replies AiMY already answered for you, so they don’t clutter your Inbox.
