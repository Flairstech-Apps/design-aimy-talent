Not every call happens inside AiMY. If you called someone from your own mobile, on a WhatsApp call or on Teams, you log it here. You pick the person, and you can search by name or role. The outcomes aren’t just labels: “Passed the screen” moves them to the next stage, “Call back” puts them back in your list, “Not a fit” takes them out, exactly like a call made inside AiMY. If they agreed to be recorded, you can upload the recording and AiMY reads it the same way.
