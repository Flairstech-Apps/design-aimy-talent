Two choices, five ways to make them

The Reports page has two filters: when the requisitions opened (last 30, 60 or 90 days) and which client account. Today they are two rows of pills that wrap onto three lines, before the story even starts. Each option below is a working mock: press the controls. They are drawn with the page’s real counts (Omar Saeed, 4 Nov) and sit above the real headline, because the filters exist to change it. Option 1 is the recommendation.

What is wrong

  • Ten pills of equal weight for two decisions; the account row alone wraps.
  • The scope is said twice: in the line under the title and again in the pills.
  • It grows with every new client; at twelve accounts it is four lines.
  • Two filter rows breaks the page’s own rule: one filter row per page.

What must stay

  • The count beside each choice: it says whether a view has enough to read.
  • The view in the link, so a view can be sent.
  • Changing either one rewrites the headline and chapters.
1 · Recommended

The sentence: the scope line becomes the filter

The line under the title already says the scope in words. Make its two blanks pressable and the filter row disappears. Each blank opens a short menu with the counts. The story reads straight on from it.

Works

  • No filter row at all: the scope line that was already there does the job.
  • The page reads as one story, from what it covers to what happened.
  • Grows with clients inside the menu, never across the page.

Falls short

  • The counts are only seen once a menu opens.
  • The underline has to be clear enough on dark; a dashed accent line with a chevron.
  • Harder to translate: word order changes between languages.

The analytics standard. Three periods are exactly what a segmented control is for, and the counts stay visible on it. The accounts move into one menu that lists each with its count.

5

Ask AiMY: say the view, confirm the change

No control on the page at all, beyond a tag saying the scope. You type “Northwind, last 30 days” in the AiMY bar; AiMY answers with QA’s filter card, the changes it read as chips, and Apply. The tag on the page shows the view and clears it. A companion to 1 rather than a replacement.

Considered and set aside

A date picker with presets and Compare (Wix, Mintlify, Mixpanel): a calendar invites ranges the three windows do not support; Compare is worth adding only if the story wants “against the last 30 days”. Tokens with Add filter (Grain): built for open-ended filtering, and it would hide the period, which always applies. Saved views (Qatalog, Ashby): three periods times six accounts is eighteen views, too few to need saving; the link already keeps the view.

Recommendation

Option 1, with option 5 behind it

Why the sentence

  • The page is now a story; its first line should be a sentence, not a toolbar.
  • It removes the second filter row the page’s own rule forbids, and the duplicate scope line.
  • Menus carry the counts, from Revolut’s account list and Amplitude’s periods.
  • The same blanks can sit in the recap and the shared link, so a sent view reads the same.

And AiMY behind it

  • Typing “Northwind, last 30 days” in the bar applies the same two blanks, through QA’s filter card.
  • The chapter questions already open in the canvas; asking for a view is the same move.
  • If the sentence tests as too hidden, fall back to option 2: it keeps the same menus.
