Candidates

Evidence you can check

  • “3 of 4 must-haves”, never a match %, one dot per must-have
  • Unknown is said as unknown, not as missing
  • CV says next to interview found, on one card
  • A reason on Not progressing; returning people flagged
  • Search, one requisition, cards or a list
Every candidate is scored as a count, three of four must-haves, never a percentage. Opaque scores are a legal target, and research shows people follow an AI pick about nine times in ten even when it is wrong, so the evidence is built to be checked. A must-have the CV is silent on reads as not known, not as missing, and it comes with a way to find out: ask the candidate, or check it in the next interview. On the profile, each must-have shows the CV quote and what the interview found, with one status: confirmed, or differs. That is the verified skills record nobody else keeps.
