The client desk

A reason on every “no”

  • The client sees only the profiles we send, with a write-up we approved
  • Meet them, or pass with one reason from a short list
  • Reasons add up: “most declines here mention spoken German”
  • AiMY proposes a change; the HR Manager approves it
  • Never salary, margin or internal notes
This is the first loop. The client has their own desk and sees only the profiles we send, each with a write-up the recruiter approved. They meet someone, or pass with one reason from a short list: spoken language, experience, salary, notice, skills. None of the eight staffing tools we studied records this. Once the reasons add up, AiMY can say most of this client’s declines mention spoken German, and propose to check spoken German on the phone screen before a profile goes to them. An HR Manager approves that change; nothing changes on its own. The client never sees salary, margin or our notes.
