One thing is urgent and is not a design decision. Egypt’s data protection law has a one-year grace period that ends around the first of November: FlairsTech needs a licence, a registered data protection officer, and a permit to move client data across borders. Please take it to legal this week. The rest is built in. Scores are evidence a person checks, and nobody is rejected without a person. We ask consent before contact, before recording, and before AI scoring. WhatsApp only after an opt-in, no LinkedIn scraping, and we never infer emotion from a face or a voice, which the EU AI Act bans. And a candidate can delete their details.
