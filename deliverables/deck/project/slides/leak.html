Finding 1

The client isn’t the main leak

What we assumed

3.4

profiles sent for every client interview. Agencies usually need 3 to 5, so a 30% acceptance is normal. Clients then hired 21 of the 24 they met.

Where we really lose

268 → 81

Only 30% of the people we screen ever reach a client, and the first one takes 14 days. Upstream, and in time.

Weak benchmark: vendor blogs and a 2018 Bullhorn survey. Treat it as direction, not proof.

Our first instinct was that clients reject too much: only 30% of profiles get an interview. But staffing firms usually send 3 to 5 profiles per client interview, and we send 3.4, so that is normal. And clients hired 21 of the 24 people they met. The real losses are upstream, where 268 screened become 81 sent, and in time, 14 days before the client sees anyone. The benchmark is weak, so treat it as a direction.
