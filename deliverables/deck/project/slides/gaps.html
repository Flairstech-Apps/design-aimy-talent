Single steps are solved. The loop isn’t.

1 · A reason when a client says no

Clients pass with a rating or free text. None of 8 staffing tools records why in a form matching can learn from.

2 · Skills verified across interviews

Tools tag evidence inside one interview. None keeps “CV says” beside “interview found” over time.

3 · Questions tied to outcomes

Vendors show pass rates per question. None checks which questions predict who clients accept.

4 · Quality of hire at day 90

Only 20% of US organisations measured it in 2025. Almost nobody feeds it back into matching.

R1–R8: ATS, staffing tools, AI sourcing, assessments, interview intelligence, insights, market, compliance · SHRM 2025

We researched eight areas and about 45 products: applicant tracking systems, staffing tools, AI sourcing, assessments and AI interviewers, interview notetakers, analytics, the market, and compliance. The single steps are crowded and well funded. What nobody does is join them into a loop that learns. Four gaps stand out: nobody records why a client says no; nobody keeps a skills record that interviews confirm over time; nobody checks which test questions predict who clients accept; and almost nobody measures who is still employed at day 90 and feeds it back.
