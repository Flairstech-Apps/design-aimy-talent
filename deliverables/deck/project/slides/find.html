Finding people starts from what is most common for us: someone like the person who left. Their role’s must-haves become the criteria, shown before the search, and the recruiter can change any line and mark it required or preferred. We search our own pool first, people who applied before and agreed to hear about roles, then public web pages. Never LinkedIn scraping; SignalHire stays parked. Every web fact shows its page and the day it was read, and anyone can report wrong data. A web person is a prospect until they reply, and first contact needs a person’s approval. Saved searches run again each time and count new people in the pool.
