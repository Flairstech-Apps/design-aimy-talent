Calls

A call, logged in a sentence

  • Each row says what to get: notice, salary, what the CV is silent on
  • A brief before, a read-back after: AiMY hears it, you confirm
  • You place every call; callbacks in the window they asked for
  • No answer: one short message, shown before it goes
  • In a run, AiMY coaches one habit at a time
The phone screen is where most people are lost, so Calls is built for speed. Each row says what to get from that call: their notice, the salary they expect, and the must-have their CV is silent on. Pressing Call opens a brief; hanging up, AiMY reads back what it heard, I read that as passed the screen, is that right?, and logging is one press, or one sentence to correct it. The recruiter places every call; nothing dials by itself. A callback has the window the person asked for, like this evening after six. After no answer, one short message names the role. And in a run of calls, AiMY coaches one habit at a time, the way AiMY Sales does.
