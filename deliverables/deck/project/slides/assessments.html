Assessments follow what the research says keeps people finishing and keeps it fair. Each test carries a checklist: about ten to fifteen minutes, workable on a phone, a practice question first, and a short note to everyone after. Integrity, like an answer pasted in, is said on its own line and never changes the score; a person looks. The German check gives a level from A1 to C2, because our requisitions ask for German at C1. Before anything is scored, the candidate is told AiMY scores and a person decides, and can choose a person instead. And for each question we show whether it predicts who clients accept, once there is enough data.
