Today

A briefing, not a list

  • Decisions waiting on you, oldest first, each one press with Undo
  • What waiting costs, in days past the usual time
  • One move on a requisition that will miss its date
  • Prepare me before each interview
  • The answer owed to a candidate, shown before it goes
This is where a recruiter starts the day. Not a list of records, a briefing: four decisions wait on you, the oldest three days; two interviews today; one requisition likely to miss its date, and why. Each decision is one press, and each can be undone. Below it, each block shows three items and what waiting costs. Research showed 46 of 105 lost days were spent waiting for feedback, so we show it in days. Before an interview, Prepare me writes the brief: what to probe, what is already confirmed. And when someone is not progressing, the answer they will get is shown before it is sent.
