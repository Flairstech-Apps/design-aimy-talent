One definition per number

  • Every measure says its unit, start, stop and what it counts
  • A target and a month-by-month trend on each
  • Staffing: profiles per hire, declined offers, day 90
  • Where time goes: days lost, by step and who it waits on
  • Team views only; no ranking of recruiters
Today’s dashboard says 21 hired in one place and 13 in another: two units under one label. So every number here carries one definition, its unit, where it starts and stops, and what it counts, behind How it is counted. Each has a target and a month-by-month trend, filtered by period and account. We added the staffing measures the dashboard lacks: profiles per hire, declined offers, people hired but not started, and billed hires next to who stayed to day 90. Where time goes shows the days lost by step and by who it waits on. And it shows team views only: research found ranking people cut productivity, so individual coaching lives in AiMY QA.
