  • At this pace: the likely date against the target
  • First profile against the 7-day target
  • Start from Replace someone who left, or a briefing
  • Approval only for exceptions: a new seat, over the band, 3+ seats, unbilled
  • Each client’s own stage template
Each requisition card now says when it is likely to fill at this pace, against the date we promised, and how many days the first profile took against a seven-day target. 82% of our requests replace someone who left, so a new requisition starts there: pick the person, and the role, must-haves and account come with them. Or paste the notes from the kickoff call, and AiMY drafts it and says what the notes did not say. Opening it needs no approval unless it is an exception: a new seat, a budget above its band, three or more seats, or an unbilled client role. Then only the person who answers for that exception is asked.
