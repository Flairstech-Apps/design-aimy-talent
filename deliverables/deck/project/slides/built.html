Rules everywhere: one press with Undo · evidence as counts · unknown said as unknown · a person decides · Sales’s components

This is what we built. AiMY Talent sits on the AiMY Sales shell and components, so it looks and behaves like the rest of the AiMY family. It has five desks. The recruiter, Omar, does the daily work across eight tabs. The hiring manager, Rania, gives debriefs and approves offers. Dina, the HR Manager, sees every requisition and approves what AiMY proposes to learn. Hesham, the BU Director, answers for budget and headcount. And Jana, at the client, sees only the profiles we send and decides with a reason. Everywhere, the same rules apply: one press with Undo, no confirm dialogs, evidence as counts, unknown said as unknown, and a person making every decision.
