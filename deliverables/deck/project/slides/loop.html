The loop we close

Six steps, and three loops back into finding

1

Request

Client request, requisition, stage template

2

Find

Pool first, then web prospects

3

Judge

Test, phone screen, HR and technical

4

Client

Profile sent, client interview, a reason

5

Offer, join

Parallel approval, accepted, joined

6

Day 90

Check-ins, retention

Client feedback → matching

A reason on every client decision teaches what fit means for that client.

Interviews → the candidate

Each interview confirms or contradicts the CV, so the next role starts from what we know.

Day 90 → success profile

Check-ins feed changes an HR Manager approves, once there are 20 of them.

This is the loop we close, in six steps: the request, finding people, judging them, the client, the offer and joining, and day 90. Three things feed back. First, every client decision carries a reason, so matching learns what fit means for that client. Second, every interview confirms or contradicts what the CV says, so the next requisition starts from what we already know. Third, day-90 check-ins feed changes to the success profile, which an HR Manager approves, and only once there are enough of them, at least 20.
