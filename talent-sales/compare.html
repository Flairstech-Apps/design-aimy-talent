AiMY Sales and AiMY Talent

Talent is the same product as Sales doing a different job: a hiring loop instead of a sales loop. This page lines the two up, and the prototype next to it takes Sales's UX as it is, with recruiting words and the research's guardrails added.

Open the Talent prototypeOpen AiMY Sales

The short version

Sales

A caller works a queue of people, calls them, and AiMY reads each call back. Leads climb a ladder to "Handed over"; a manager turns them into deals. Campaigns set the goal. The client sees promises kept, never the people.

Talent

A recruiter works a queue of candidates, phone-screens them, and AiMY reads each call back. Candidates climb a stage template to "Joined"; the client decides on profiles sent. Requisitions set the goal. The client sees evidence, never salary or our notes.

The rule from the vault: "A new product is a new process on the existing design, not a new design." Everything below marked Same is copied from Sales unchanged in the prototype.

The two ladders, side by side

Not called→No answer→Callback→Answered→Meeting set→Showed up→Interested→Handed over
Sourced→Applied→Assessment→Phone screen→HR→Technical→Profile sent→Client interview→Offer→Accepted→Joined

Exits in Sales: Declined, Wrong number, Do not call. Exits in Talent: Not progressing, Withdrew, Client declined, Offer declined. Neither is a step.

What Talent adds

On the record

  • Success profile and evidence per criterion
  • CV says beside Interview found
  • Consent with dates
  • The sendout as a record: sent, opened, decided
  • Day-30 and day-90 check-ins

Loops that learn

  • One reason on every client Pass
  • Learning proposals the HR Manager approves
  • Questions tied to client outcomes after 20
  • What AiMY may do, on three rungs

People outside the desks

  • The careers page, applying through AiMY
  • The candidate's own profile, by one-time code, in Arabic too
  • The interviewer's debrief link
  • The approvers' link

Guardrails

  • Everyone hears within 5 days
  • No LinkedIn scraping; SignalHire parked
  • No ranking of recruiters
  • Honest reports: where people are lost upstream

What stays in Sales

The money apparatus (Talent keeps only billable hires), calling runs and high-volume dialling, LinkedIn connections, account tiers (Gold, Silver, Bench), and per-person performance, which belongs in AiMY QA.
